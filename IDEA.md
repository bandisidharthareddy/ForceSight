## 1. 🎯 Executive Summary & Vision

**ForceSight** is a proactive computer vision and predictive crowd pressure simulation system engineered to eliminate crowd surges, stampedes, and crushing incidents at structural bottlenecks (gates, barricades, turnstiles, and narrow passageways) during high-density events such as college fests, sports events, concerts, and temple gatherings.

Current event safety relies on subjective headcounts and reactive human observations. In reality, compressive mechanical pressure builds up invisibly within dense crowds—often pinning individuals at the front against barriers while those in the rear remain unaware. **ForceSight makes this invisible force visible in real time.** By translating video density analytics into an estimated mechanical force metric ($kg$), ForceSight gives security marshals an indisputable, color-coded early-warning dashboard to throttle ingress or divert crowds before dangerous thresholds are reached.

## 2. 🔍 The Problem Space

### 2.1 The "Invisible Danger" Phenomenon
* **Mechanical Pressure Transfer:** At densities exceeding 4–5 people/$\text{m}^2$, compressive force transfers across bodies like a shockwave. People at the front can experience fatal compressive asphyxia at loads upwards of $250\text{–}400\text{ kg}$, even while the crowd appears calm on the periphery.
* **Line-of-Sight Blind Spots:** On-ground security personnel at gate perimeters cannot inspect density surges or bottlenecks developing 20–30 meters back in the crowd queue.
* **The Reactive Delay Trap:** Security interventions historically happen only after distress cues appear (e.g., screaming, falling, physical barricade buckling). At that stage, crowd momentum makes immediate de-escalation nearly impossible.
